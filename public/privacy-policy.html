Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2025

Empyreal Keys is an ad-free piano app from Empyreal Works. The app works without a user account.

Data on your device

Piano settings and recordings are stored on your device. You can delete recordings in the app or remove local app data in your device settings.

Online downloads and app verification

Empyreal Keys uses Google Firebase Storage to deliver soundfont downloads and Firebase App Check to protect that service. On Android, App Check uses Google Play Integrity to verify requests. Google processes network and app or device integrity information to deliver files and protect the service. See Google’s Firebase privacy information for details on how Firebase handles this information.

Retention

Local app data stays on your device until you delete it or clear the app’s data. For questions about service-side data or retention, contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if app functionality changes. Updates will be posted here.
